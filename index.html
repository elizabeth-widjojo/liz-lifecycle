Consumer marketplaceRETENTION / 01
~5 pp
recovery in new-user retention over the initiative period

Recovering early-life retention

A broad treatment strategy was missing customers' different likelihoods of returning, making retention spending less efficient.

Read the approach
Challenge

New-user cohorts were dropping off and blanket incentives provided limited precision.

Approach

Collaborated with analysts on behavioral segmentation, compared intervention types and tested targeted incentives against controls.

Reported result

Retention recovered by approximately five percentage points during the wider initiative period. This is a before/after recovery figure, not a claim that the full change was caused by one experiment.

Digital consumer serviceRE-ENGAGEMENT / 02
+18%
reported bookings uplift from a re-engagement test

Finding the right moment to re-engage

Generic reminders were giving the same treatment to customers with very different likelihoods of returning.

Read the approach
Challenge

One-size-fits-all messaging lacked customer-level timing and relevance.

Approach

Worked with a data team to compare predictive approaches, test communication channels and optimize reminder timing.

Reported result

An internal experiment reported 18% higher bookings. Results reflect the original scope and measurement period rather than a universal expected lift.

Consumer appREPEAT USAGE / 03
+9.5%
monthly booking frequency versus control

Making repeat usage more rewarding

An active-user segment needed a more relevant reason to return without relying on undifferentiated promotions.

Read the approach
Challenge

Repeat-usage frequency was flattening across an established user base.

Approach

Designed segment-level challenges, aligned incentives with behavior and iterated the program against a control group.

Reported result

The initiative reported 9.5% higher monthly booking frequency versus control. The result is specific to the experiment and context.

Digital productAI ONBOARDING / 04
>80%
query resolution in an early, limited sample

Bringing on-demand help into onboarding

Prospective first-time customers had questions at the exact moment they needed help to move forward.

Read the approach
Challenge

Key onboarding moments lacked readily available, contextual answers.

Approach

Designed a knowledge-guided conversational agent, integrated entry points into lifecycle messaging and added routes to human help for sensitive issues.

Reported result

An early, limited sample indicated over 80% of queries resolved without human involvement. This does not establish a statistically proven increase in customer activation.
